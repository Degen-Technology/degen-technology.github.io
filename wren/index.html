Questions, answered

How is this different from other period apps?

Standard trackers assume regular cycles and predict one date. If your cycles vary — as they often do with PCOS — that date is misleading. Wren predicts a range and tells you how confident it is.

Is it a medical device?

No. Wren is a tracking and information tool, not a diagnosis. Talk to your clinician about medical decisions.
